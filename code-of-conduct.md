# Code of Conduct

We represent Chelsea every time we put the kit on. These are the standards, and they
apply to everyone — players, staff, and volunteers.

## On the pitch
- **Respect the referee.** The captain talks to officials; everyone else plays on.
  Dissent costs us cards and games.
- **Respect opponents.** Compete hard, play fair, shake hands after.
- **Discipline.** Reckless or violent play, and abuse of any kind, is never worth it.
- **Team first.** No individual is bigger than the team. Celebrate together, take
  responsibility together.

## Off the pitch
- **Commitment.** Turn up to training and matches on time and ready. Tell the coach
  in advance if you can't make it.
- **Preparation.** Rest, hydrate, and look after your body. You owe the team your best
  condition.
- **Represent the club well** — in person and online. What you post reflects on all of us.

## Non-negotiables
- No discrimination, harassment, or bullying of any kind — on the pitch, in the
  changing room, or online. Zero tolerance.
- No alcohol or recreational drugs around matches or training.
- Safeguarding comes first for any youth players; concerns go straight to the
  designated welfare officer.

## Consequences
Breaches are handled by the coaching staff, proportionate to the issue — from a quiet
word, to being dropped, to removal from the squad for serious cases.

We keep each other to these standards. That's what makes us a team.
