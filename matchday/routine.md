# Match Day

The timeline below is for a standard kickoff. Times are relative to kickoff (KO).

## Before the match
- **KO − 90 min:** Arrive at the ground. Hand in kit, settle in, no phones once the
  team meeting starts.
- **KO − 75 min:** Team meeting. Line-up, opposition report, set-piece assignments,
  three key messages for the day.
- **KO − 55 min:** Warm-up begins (led by fitness coach): activation → passing →
  positional → finishing → sprints.
- **KO − 20 min:** Back in, rehydrate, final words, boots and shin pads checked.
- **KO − 5 min:** Out for kickoff. Focused, together, loud.

## During the match
- **Stick to the plan** until it's changed. If something isn't working, communicate —
  don't go rogue.
- **Talk constantly.** Organizing, warning, encouraging. A quiet team is a
  disorganized team.
- **Restart fast when we're chasing, slow it down when we're protecting a lead.**
- **Half-time:** two minutes to breathe and rehydrate, then adjustments. Listen.

## Roles for the day
- **Captain:** sets the tone, talks to the referee, rallies the team.
- **Vice-captain:** organizes the defense / back line.
- **Set-piece leaders:** confirm assignments before each dead ball.

## After the match
- Warm-down and stretch — win or lose.
- Shake hands with opponents and the officials. We win and lose with class.
- Kit and equipment collected. Nothing left behind.
- Brief reflection now; the full review is Monday. No heated post-mortems in the heat
  of the moment.
