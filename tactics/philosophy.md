# Playing Philosophy

Everything we do on the pitch comes back to four ideas. If you're ever unsure what
to do, default to these.

## 1. Defend as a unit
Defending is not the back four's job — it's all eleven. When we lose the ball, the
nearest player presses, the next players screen passing lanes, and everyone shuffles
across to keep the team compact. No one is a spectator.

## 2. Win the ball high
We'd rather win the ball 40 yards from their goal than 40 yards from ours. Our
pressing triggers (see below) tell us *when* to go. When one person presses, the
rest follow — a half-hearted press by one player just gets us bypassed.

## 3. Move it fast
The ball moves faster than any player can run. One and two-touch play in tight
areas, quick switches to the free side, and forward passes whenever they're on. We
don't slow the game down to admire it.

## 4. Big when we have it, small when we don't
**In possession:** stretch the pitch — wide players hug the touchline, we use the
full width and depth to create space.
**Out of possession:** compress — narrow the gaps between lines and between players
so there's nowhere to play through us.

## Pressing triggers — when to go
Press hard when you see any of these:
- A bad or heavy first touch by an opponent.
- A backward or square pass.
- A player receiving with their back to our goal.
- A pass into a player we've numbered up against near the touchline.

If none of these are present, hold your shape and stay patient.
