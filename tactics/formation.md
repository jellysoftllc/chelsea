# Formation

## Base shape: 4-3-3

```
              GK
        LB  CB  CB  RB
             DM
          CM     CM
     LW      CF      RW
```

Our default is a 4-3-3 with a single holding midfielder (DM) and two more advanced
central midfielders (CM). It gives us a stable spine, width from the wingers, and
numbers to press.

## In possession (build-up)
- The two center-backs split wide, the DM drops between or just ahead of them to
  make a back-three shape and beat the first line of pressure.
- Full-backs push high and wide to give width, OR tuck inside ("invert") to
  overload midfield — the coach will call which on the day.
- The two CMs stagger: one shows for the ball, one runs beyond.
- Wingers stay high and wide to pin their full-backs; the center-forward occupies
  both center-backs.

## Out of possession
- We drop into a **4-5-1 / 4-1-4-1**: wingers tuck back alongside the midfield three,
  the center-forward leads the press.
- Two banks of players, compact, roughly 25–30 yards between the defensive and
  midfield lines.

## Variations we can switch to
- **4-2-3-1** — add a second holding midfielder against strong central opponents;
  gives more protection and a clear No. 10.
- **3-4-3 / back three** — when we want an extra body in build-up and attacking
  full-backs (wing-backs); good against a two-striker press.

The base shape is the default. Only change on the coach's call or a pre-agreed
in-game trigger.
