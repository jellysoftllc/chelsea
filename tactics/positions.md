# Positions — Roles & Responsibilities

What each position is expected to do. "In possession" = we have the ball,
"Out of possession" = they have it.

## Goalkeeper (GK)
- **In possession:** start our build-up, be a calm passing option, play out under
  pressure when it's on, go long when it's not. Sweep behind the high line.
- **Out of possession:** command your box, organize the wall, communicate the offside
  line loudly.

## Center-backs (CB x2)
- **In possession:** split wide in build-up, step into midfield with the ball when
  space allows, hit diagonal switches to the far winger.
- **Out of possession:** defend the space in behind first, win your duels, hold the
  line together — step up and drop as a pair, never split.

## Full-backs (LB / RB)
- **In possession:** provide width high up, overlap or underlap the winger, deliver
  early crosses. Invert into midfield when called.
- **Out of possession:** don't get caught up the pitch — recovery runs are
  non-negotiable. Defend the winger, show them onto their weaker foot.

## Defensive midfielder (DM)
- **In possession:** the pivot. Always available, always facing forward. Recycle and
  redistribute; rarely the one who scores.
- **Out of possession:** screen the space in front of the back four, break up the
  first wave of attacks, cover when a CB steps out.

## Central midfielders (CM x2)
- **In possession:** connect defense and attack, arrive late in the box, one goes
  while one stays (never both forward at once).
- **Out of possession:** press the opposition midfield, track runners all the way,
  win second balls.

## Wingers (LW / RW)
- **In possession:** stay high and wide to stretch the pitch, take on your defender
  1v1, cut inside to shoot or overlap to combine.
- **Out of possession:** press their full-back on the trigger, tuck in to make a
  five when we're defending deep.

## Center-forward (CF)
- **In possession:** lead the line, pin the center-backs, hold the ball up and bring
  others in, get on the end of crosses.
- **Out of possession:** start the press, set its direction (show them one way), cut
  the passing lane between their center-backs.
