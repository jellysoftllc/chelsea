# Set Pieces

Set pieces win tight games. Know your job on every one — assignments are posted
before each match, but these are the defaults.

## Attacking corners
- **Delivery:** inswinger to the near post as first choice; outswinger to the penalty
  spot as the variation.
- **Runners:** two attack the near post, two attack the far post, one holds at the
  penalty spot, one stays on the edge of the box for the cutback / second ball.
- **Rest defense:** two players stay back on the halfway line against the counter,
  plus the DM screening.

## Attacking free kicks (wide)
- Treat like a corner — same near/far/spot runs.
- Have a short option ready every time to keep them honest.

## Attacking free kicks (central, shooting range)
- Designated striker takes it. One dummy runner over the ball. One player on the end
  of the wall for a deflection / rebound.

## Defending corners
- **Zonal + man mix:** two players zone the near post and six-yard area; the rest
  man-mark their biggest threats. GK commands the whole box.
- One player on each post (or one, per the day's plan).
- One player stays high up the pitch as the out-ball for our counter.

## Defending free kicks
- Wall size called by the GK. Line up quickly — the biggest danger is a fast free kick
  before we're set.
- Mark up early, hold the line for offside, communicate constantly.

## Throw-ins
- **Attacking third:** look for the long throw into the box if we have a specialist;
  otherwise quick and short to keep possession.
- **Defensive third:** never a risky square throw across our own box. Down the line
  or back to the GK.
