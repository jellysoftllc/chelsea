# Weekly Training Schedule

A typical week built around a Saturday match. Sessions adjust for midweek fixtures —
the coach will publish changes.

| Day | Focus | Intensity | Notes |
|-----|-------|-----------|-------|
| **Mon** | Recovery + review | Low | Light session, mobility, match video review. Optional for those who played 90'. |
| **Tue** | Physical + possession | High | Fitness base, rondos, positional possession games. |
| **Wed** | Tactical (attacking) | Medium–High | Build-up patterns, final-third combinations, finishing. |
| **Thu** | Tactical (defending) | Medium | Pressing triggers, defensive shape, set-piece defending. |
| **Fri** | Activation + set pieces | Low–Medium | Sharpness, set-piece routines, match briefing. |
| **Sat** | **MATCH DAY** | — | See [matchday/routine.md](../matchday/routine.md). |
| **Sun** | Off | — | Rest and recover. |

## Standing rules
- **Arrive 15 minutes early** and ready to start on time.
- **Report injuries and illness immediately** — don't hide a knock.
- Every session starts with a proper warm-up and ends with a cool-down. No shortcuts.
- Hydrate before, during, and after. Bring your own bottle.
- Bring both training kit and boots for grass and astro.
